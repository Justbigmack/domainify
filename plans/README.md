## Findings considered and rejected

- **Push `'use client'` below `DomainsTable`/`DomainHeader`** (audit
  BUNDLE-03): MED-risk refactor whose payoff scales with domain count;
  defensible as-is at this app's data volume. Revisit if users hold hundreds
  of domains.
- **Hoist per-row dropdown/dialog instances in the domain lists** (audit
  RENDER-02): same reasoning — structural smell, negligible practical cost at
  current scale.
- **Merge `VerifySteps`' inline `Step` into the `Stepper` compound**: the two
  step visuals may be intentionally different; needs a design decision before
  any refactor — investigate, don't fix.
- **File-size (250-line) cleanup**: no hand-written file exceeds the limit;
  the two files near it are vendored shadcn primitives. Not worth doing.
